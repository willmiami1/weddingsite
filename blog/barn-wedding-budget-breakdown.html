What a Central Florida Barn Wedding Really Costs: A Realistic Budget Breakdown

Destiny Ranch Events · Bushnell, Florida

Wedding budgets fall apart when couples guess. So let's not guess — here's a transparent look at what an affordable barn wedding in Central Florida actually costs, using real venue pricing and honest vendor ranges from the weddings we host at Destiny Ranch Events.

Start with the venue — and read the fine print

Our pricing is flat and public:

  • Saturday: $4,995
  • Friday or Sunday: $3,995
  • Weekday: $2,995

The number that matters, though, is what's included. At many venues, the quoted price is just the door key — then come the add-ons. Our base price includes taxes, cleaning, trash removal, tables and chairs for up to 180 guests, private dressing suites, sound system, the LED screen, and use of our full decor collection. When you compare venues, compare the all-in number, not the headline.

The big three: catering, photography, bar

  • Catering: $25–60 per guest for quality buffet or family-style service in our area. Because we allow any caterer (or your own cooking in the commercial kitchen), food trucks and family recipes can cut this dramatically.
  • Photography: $2,500–4,500 for an experienced Central Florida wedding photographer. This is the vendor most couples say not to cut.
  • Bar: $1,500–2,000 if you BYO with a licensed bartender — versus $3,750+ for typical package bars. See the full math in our BYO alcohol guide.

A sample budget: 100 guests on a Friday

  • Venue (Friday, all-in): $3,995
  • Catering (100 × $35): $3,500
  • Photography: $3,000
  • BYO bar + licensed bartender: $1,700
  • DJ: $1,200
  • Flowers & personal decor extras: $1,500
  • Attire, rings, hair & makeup: $2,500
  • Cake, invitations, misc.: $1,100

Total: about $18,500 — well under the Florida average of $30,000+, without cutting the guest experience. Move to a weekday and trim florals, and couples regularly land near $15,000.

Where barn venues quietly save you money

  • The setting is the decor. Oak trees, pastures, string lights, and weathered wood mean you need far fewer flowers to make it feel finished.
  • Included inventory. Tables, chairs, and our decor collection replace rental line items that add up fast.
  • One venue, both events. Ceremony and reception in one place removes shuttle and double-rental costs.
  • Sunset is free. The best lighting design money can buy is scheduled, not purchased — see our golden hour photo guide.
